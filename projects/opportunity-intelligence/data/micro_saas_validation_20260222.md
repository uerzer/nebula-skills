## Validation Framework Methodology

This framework evaluates micro SaaS opportunities across 10 weighted dimensions:

1. **Market Demand** (15%): Combines TAM size, growth indicators, and urgency signals
2. **Monetization** (15%): Pricing power, revenue potential, and payment willingness
3. **Competition** (12%): Market gaps, forced choices, differentiation potential
4. **Distribution** (12%): Multi-category reach, customer acquisition channels
5. **Build Complexity** (10%): Time to MVP, feature complexity (inverse scored)
6. **Timing** (10%): Structural gaps, market readiness, urgency windows
7. **Moat** (8%): Feature defensibility, switching costs, network effects
8. **Founder Fit** (8%): Solo-buildable, technical complexity, domain needs
9. **Scalability** (6%): Margin structure, automation potential
10. **Risk** (4%): Regulatory, execution, market risks (inverse scored)

Each dimension scored 0-100, then weighted and summed for final validation score.

- 80-100: STRONG VALIDATE - High-confidence opportunity, pursue immediately
- 70-79: VALIDATE - Solid opportunity, validate assumptions then build
- 60-69: CONDITIONAL - Needs deeper validation, risky but possible
