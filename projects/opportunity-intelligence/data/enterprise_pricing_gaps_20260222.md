## KEY TAKEAWAYS

1. **The $50-150 SMB gap is the highest-ROI opportunity** - Most SaaS tools jump from $20-50 Pro to $500+ Enterprise, leaving small businesses without options.

2. **Commitment discounts are low-hanging fruit** - Almost no one offers quarterly/biannual billing, yet it would convert annual-hesitant buyers.

3. **Hybrid pricing reduces churn** - Base + consumption models prevent both over-provisioning waste and bill-shock surprises.

4. **Agencies are massively underserved** - Multi-client management requires cobbling together separate accounts or overpaying for enterprise.

5. **Solo-to-team transition is painful** - $10 individual plan → $100+ team plan (5 seats × $20) kills growth for micro-agencies.

6. **Power users forced into wrong tiers** - High-volume solo users pay for team features they don't need just to get higher limits.

7. **Free-to-paid jump is too steep** - $0 → $50/month loses millions of potential customers who would pay $10-20.
