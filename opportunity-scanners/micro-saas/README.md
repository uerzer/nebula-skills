# Micro SaaS Validation Framework

Pain point discovery and ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) research for micro SaaS opportunities.

## Research Focus

- Underserved niche identification
- Recurring revenue potential
- Low competition / high willingness to pay
- Technical feasibility assessment

## Validation Signals

- Reddit/forum complaint frequency
- Existing solution gaps
- Search volume for problem keywords
- Competitor pricing analysis

## Current Status

Active scanner - researching opportunities.

## Key Metrics

- Problem severity score
- Market size estimates
- Competition density
- Build complexity rating
- Revenue potential (MRR projections)
